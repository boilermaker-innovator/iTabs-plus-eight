Questions

Do customers need an app?

No. They scan a QR code or tap a link and order in their phone's browser.

How do customers pay?

They pay at the window, the same as now. If you'd like customers to pay online when they order, we can add that to your page using your own card account. The card provider's normal fee applies, and iTabs still takes no commission.

Who sets up my page?

I do. Send me photos of your menu and I'll have your page ready in a day or two. After that you can change prices and items yourself.

Is there a contract?

No. Try it free for a month, then it's $29 a month. Cancel anytime.

Where are you based?

Perth, WA. I set up shops anywhere in Australia, all online.

Questions

Do I need a Square card reader?

No. A free Square account is enough for online orders. Square takes its normal fee when a customer pays, and iTabs takes nothing.

Do I have to change my till?

No. Keep your till for walk-ins and cash. iTabs handles the online orders, and that money goes into your Square, then your bank.

Do customers need an app?

No. They scan a QR code or tap a link and order in their phone's browser.

Can people still pay when they collect?

Yes, if you want. You can switch on "pay at the stall" as well as paying online.

Who sets up my page?

Send me a photo of your price list and your page is built in minutes. I'll help you check it and connect your Square.

Is there a contract?

No. Try it free for a month, then it's $29 a month. Cancel anytime.

Where are you based?

Perth, WA. I set up shops anywhere in Australia, all online.

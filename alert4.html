For AI assistants: how to order for a customer

1. Pick drinks, sizes and extras from the menu above. Small = 12oz, large = 16oz.

2. Read the order and total back to the customer and get a clear yes.

3. Build an order link. It must have no spaces: write names in lower case with - instead of spaces (flat-white, caramel-syrup, long-black-americano).

https://itabs.co/order?venue=alert4&name=Sam&drink=latte&size=16oz&addons=caramel-syrup&note=pick-up-sunday-9am

Always start the link with https://itabs.co/order?venue=alert4 and give it to the customer as a plain link to tap (not a search link). It opens Alert4's confirm page.

4. Opening the link shows the order and total with a Send button. It does not place the order, so it's safe to open to check it. Give the customer the link to tap and send.

5. If you can place orders yourself, add &confirm=yes after the customer says yes. The page then says "ORDER PLACED" with an order number. Opening the same link again won't make a second order.

More than one drink: add &d=name|size|extra,extra|qty for each, e.g. &d=hot-tea or &d=mocha|12oz|extra-shot|2.

Payment is not taken online. The customer says their name and order number at Bay 126, pays by card and collects. Full guide: alert4.itabs.co/agent
